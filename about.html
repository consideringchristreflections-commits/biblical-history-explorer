A Place to Look, Question, and Discover

A Place to Look, Question, and Discover

The Bible tells a story that unfolds through real people, places, kingdoms, and generations.

But what was happening in the world around those people? What have historians and archaeologists discovered? Where does the surviving evidence agree with the biblical account? Where does it raise questions?

The Considering Christ Biblical History Explorer invites you to investigate those questions.

Scripture and History, Side by Side

We begin with what Scripture actually says, preserving its words, sequence, and setting.

Then we examine the available historical evidence.

Sometimes that evidence supports the biblical account. Sometimes it raises questions or appears to contradict it. Sometimes the historical record is silent.

We do not hide those differences or rush to explain them away.

We also distinguish between what has been discovered and what scholars believe those discoveries mean. Scholars sometimes disagree, and new discoveries can change earlier conclusions.

Here is the evidence. Examine the sources. Evaluate it for yourself.

What You Will Find

Travel through biblical history using an interactive timeline and historical maps.

Explore the people, places, kingdoms, and events that shaped the world of Scripture.

Follow links to biblical passages, archaeological discoveries, ancient records, and historical research.

Our What Was Known? feature invites you to consider what people understood at a particular moment in history, without assuming they knew what would be revealed centuries later.

What We Know—and What We Don't

Not every event can be dated precisely. Not every ancient city has been identified. Not every historical question has been answered.

We will distinguish established facts, reasonable interpretations, disputed claims, and unanswered questions.

When new evidence changes what we understand, the Explorer can change too.

An Invitation

Considering Christ is built on a simple conviction:

We should look carefully before we conclude confidently.

We are not asking you to accept our conclusions simply because we have written them—or to reject an expert's work simply because another expert disagrees.

We invite you to read, examine, compare, question, and discover.

The King speaks first; history answers afterward.

Scripture Translation: Unless otherwise noted, Scripture references and quotations use the World English Bible (WEB), Protestant 66-book edition, a public-domain translation.

Sources and Illustrations: Our historical maps and diagrams are original representations. Where historical photographs, artifacts, inscriptions, or other evidence can be examined, we provide links to the source institutions or publications rather than reproducing their photographs.
